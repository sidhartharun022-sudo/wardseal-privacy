Ruseward Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026. Ruseward was previously called Wardseal; this policy covers both names.

1. What Ruseward does

Ruseward is a browser extension that warns you about online scams and lets you keep messages and files private. It warns about scam and phishing sites, pirated streaming and download sites, risky links and downloads, and scam signs in chats and emails. It also lets you encrypt messages, chat privately with a friend (Private Mode), and lock photos and files with a passphrase.

2. What stays on your device

Everything. To warn you, Ruseward reads the address and content of the pages you open and the text of chats and emails on supported sites. All of this is checked inside your browser. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. Ruseward has no server of its own.

3. What is saved on your device

Ruseward keeps the following in your browser's extension storage, on your computer only:

  • Your settings: language, dark or light mode, which checks are switched on, and sites you chose to trust or switch off.
  • Private Mode: your own key, the names and keys of friends you added, and which chats have Private Mode switched on.
  • A record of which sites you have visited before, kept as scrambled fingerprints that cannot be read back, so Ruseward can tell you when a site is new to you.
  • For the "different account number" alarm: a scrambled fingerprint and the last four digits of bank account numbers people sent you. Never the full number.
  • Warnings you chose to save in your own reports list.
  • Downloaded lists of known scam and piracy sites.

Removing the extension deletes all of it.

4. Passwords, passphrases, card numbers and short-lived notes

Ruseward may look at a password you type to tell you it is weak, and at numbers in a chat to spot a one-time code or a card number being asked for. These are checked on the spot and are not saved. Passphrases you use to encrypt or decrypt are used on your device only. A passphrase you used to open a message is remembered for that browser tab until you close the browser, so other messages in the same chat can open, and is never written to disk.

5. Private Mode, encrypted messages and locked files

Messages you send in Private Mode are encrypted on your computer before the chat site receives them, using keys shared only between you and the friend you paired with. Messages and files you encrypt with a passphrase can only be opened with that passphrase. Ruseward cannot read them for you and cannot recover a lost passphrase or key. The file locker works entirely on your computer; your file and passphrase are not uploaded.

6. Downloads

Ruseward regularly downloads public lists of known scam and piracy sites from OpenPhish, URLhaus (abuse.ch), Phishing Army, HaGeZi and the Global Anti-Scam Org list, hosted on those projects' own sites, GitHub and Codeberg. These requests carry no information about you or the pages you visit. As with any download, the host can see your IP address.

7. No cloud AI, no accounts, no ads

Ruseward does not send your pages or messages to any AI service. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, share or transfer your data.
  • We do not use your data for anything other than warning you about scams and encrypting what you ask it to.
  • We do not use your data to judge creditworthiness or for lending.

9. Warnings are not guarantees

Ruseward lowers your risk but cannot catch every scam, and it can sometimes warn about a safe site. If something feels wrong, stop and check with someone you trust.

10. Children

Ruseward is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

11. Changes
